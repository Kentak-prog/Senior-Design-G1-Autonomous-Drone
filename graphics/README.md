# Graphics

Every image, GIF and video of the project, in one place (reorganised 2026-11-22).

- `images/` plots, figures, diagrams, poster images, CAD and Isaac renders
- `gifs/` animated flight replays (`Trajectory/isaac_results/3_gates/scripts/animate_runner_runs.py`)
- `videos/` Isaac Sim screen recordings, explainers and detector videos

**Names:** `YYYY-MM-DD_<course>_<what it shows or tests>`. The date is when the result was made (first commit,
or the recording date). Undated material is prefixed `diagram_`, `poster_`, `cad_`, `isaac_` or `explainer_`.
Courses: `3gate` (three_gate_isaac, 1.5 m gates), `circle` (circle3_isaac), `*-pink` (Tony's 0.864 m pink gates),
`speed-oval7` (the top-speed course). `vXaY` = plan limits v_max X m/s, a_max Y m/s². `gps` = flown on PX4's
estimate (sim GPS); `gate-vio` = our first gate-only VIO; `swift-vio` = emulated VIO + Swift drift filter;
`drift-seedN` = the emulated VIO drift used.

**Git-ignored, local only:** the Isaac screen recordings (`videos/*.webm`, ~1.3 GB, three over GitHub's 100 MB limit), the explainer videos, the stage-4 gate-CNN videos (~63 MB) the poster images except the control block diagram, the diagram SVG sources, the Isaac drone-camera test frame and the two gate-CNN check images (contact sheet, no-crop check). They
stay on the machine that recorded them. Everything else here is meant to be committed.

Scripts that make figures still write new ones into their own result folders; move them here when they are
worth keeping.
