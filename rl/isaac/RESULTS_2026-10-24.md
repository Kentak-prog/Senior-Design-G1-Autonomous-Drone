# Live Isaac Sim results: PID vs MPC vs RL (2026-10-24)

First flights of all three controllers in Isaac Sim 4.5 + Pegasus + PX4 v1.18 SITL,
through the `rl/isaac/` bridge. 54 runs: 3 tracks x 3 controllers x 3 runs x 2 gate
sources. Logs (gitignored): `rl/runs/isaac/<gates>_<controller>_<track>_r<k>/`
(`log.csv`, `summary.json`) and `rl/runs/isaac/matrix_results.csv`.

## Setup (as on a real drone)

- Controllers run in the pod's `ros2-eval` container and talk to PX4 only over
  MAVLink (offboard, collective thrust + body rates via `SET_ATTITUDE_TARGET`).
- The drone's state is PX4's own EKF estimate (`LOCAL_POSITION_NED`,
  `ATTITUDE_QUATERNION`), not simulator ground truth.
- RL uses the PyTorch/SB3 weights in `rl/runs/best` (PPO v3, 135M steps), unchanged.
- MPC is Antek's NMPC (`control/`) via `rl/isaac/mpc_ctbr.py`: predicted body rate at
  the next node + thrust, IPOPT presets that bring a solve from ~105 ms to ~7 ms.
- **Known gates:** controllers get the track file.
- **Camera gates:** controllers start from a prior map perturbed by 0.5 m per axis
  and 10 deg yaw (seeds 0/1/2 = runs 1/2/3), corrected live by the colour gate
  detector + PnP on `/iris_0/front_cam/rgb` (`gate_vision.py`, `vision_gate_map.py`).
- Gate pass/hit is always scored against the true track (the gates have no colliders).
- Calibration: `--origin-offset 0 0 0.06 --thrust-scale 0.9269`, PX4 `MC_AIRMODE=1`,
  `COM_LOW_BAT_ACT=0`. Isaac ran at 0.5x real time; PX4 time = sim time.

## Findings

1. **The bridge transfers PID and MPC almost exactly.** Isaac laps are within
   0.2 s of QuadSim for both on every track with known gates, and run-to-run spread is
   below 0.05 s.
2. **MPC runs in real time on the pod.** 6.4-8.0 ms per solve on average, 22 ms worst,
   against a 20 ms control period.
3. **Vision works.** The camera corrected prior maps that were 0.2-1.3 m off to
   0.01-0.19 m in the gate plane at crossing. With camera gates, PID kept 9/9 and its
   lap times. Frames take 3-5 ms each.
4. **RL is the fastest when it works but does not transfer.** It flew oval6 in 7.68 s,
   2.5x faster than PID, with 0.31 m margin (QuadSim 0.31). Otherwise it hits gates or
   over-tilts. The cause is measured: PX4's rate loop reaches 63% of a rate step in
   58-64 ms (yaw 110-120 ms, 40% overshoot), against 24 ms in the QuadSim it trained in.
   Its QuadSim margins are only 0.31-0.55 m, so this lag puts it 0.4-0.5 m off line at
   gate 0. slalom5 also fails in QuadSim.
5. **MPC with camera gates is limited by re-planning, not by vision.**
   - oval6 run 1: gate 1, around the first turn, was never detected before arrival (the
     camera's vertical FOV is 36 deg), so the MPC flew to the prior's 0.73 m error.
   - oval6 run 2: the map was right (0.13 m), but the MPC only re-plans when a gate
     moves > 0.3 m, and the plan restarts from rest. With its ~0.4 m tracking lag that
     clipped gate 2. The 21.76 s and 33.54 s laps are the same re-plan braking.
   - PID re-aims at the current map every tick, which is why it is unaffected.

## Problems found and fixed on the way (all in `rl/isaac/`)

- **PX4 ignores CTBR for up to 0.5 s after leaving position control.** The commander
  updates its control-mode flags at 2 Hz (switch at t=339.10 s, rate mode at
  339.54 s). Fix: `runner.ctbr_preroll`, a 1 s CTBR hover before every race.
- **The Iris weighs 1.619 kg in Isaac, not 1.5.** Each rotor link adds ~0.03 kg of
  default-density mass. The fitted thrust scale 0.9269 equals 1.5/1.619, so
  `--thrust-scale` makes the drone accelerate like the 1.5 kg the controllers assume.
  The inertia matches `QuadParams`.
- **The MPC's first `reset()` builds IPOPT for seconds.** No setpoints reached PX4
  meanwhile and its offboard failsafe fired. Fix: warm-up reset before the start phase.
- **MPC re-plans blocked the loop for seconds.** Fix: `async_replan` (background
  thread); the runner turns it on in camera mode.
- **`ros2-eval` has a 2-CPU quota but reports 20 cores.** torch, OpenCV and BLAS ran
  ~20 threads each and CFS throttling stalled the loop for 1-3 s. Fix: one thread per
  library (`runner.py`, `gate_vision.py`).
- **PX4's simulated battery triggered RTL failsafes mid-run** (climbs to 7-8 m). Fix
  for the sim: `COM_LOW_BAT_ACT=0`.
- One MPC camera run flipped the drone before these fixes. It was re-flown and the
  table holds only runs made with all fixes in place.

## Suggested next steps

1. **RL:** fine-tune `runs/best` in QuadSim with the measured PX4 rate response
   (~60 ms rise, yaw overshoot) and randomised around it, then re-fly. Also aim for
   more gate margin: the policy runs with 0.3 m.
2. **MPC with camera gates:** re-plan with a smaller threshold and from the current
   velocity (the planner only starts from rest), or track the map gate directly in the
   reference.
3. **Vision:** gates around turns are seen late with the 15 deg up-tilted 36 deg vFOV
   camera. A keep-gate-in-view term (RL) or yaw-ahead (PID/MPC) would help.
